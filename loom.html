01 · Proactive主动介入

Speaks up without an @, but doesn’t butt in不用 @ 也会接话，但不乱插话

An AI that only answers @-mentions is a tool; one that answers everything is noise. After a pause, a light decision model (Jev) reads the last 20 messages and answers several questions at once: is someone actually talking to the AI, is a question unanswered or a claim wrong, is the matter already settled, and who should handle it. Only a score above the room’s bar wakes the large model, which may still choose to stay quiet.只会被 @ 的 AI 是工具，什么都接话的 AI 是噪音。讨论停顿后，轻量决策模型 Jev 读最近 20 条消息，一次回答几个问题：有人其实在跟 AI 说话吗？有没人答的问题、说错的话？事情是不是已经聊完了？该找谁？分数过了本群门槛才唤醒大模型，被唤醒后它仍然可以不说。

Five ways to step in五种介入方式

The same Jev call also picks how同一次 Jev 判断里一起决定用哪种

  • Say it插话only with a definite answer or a correction; @ the right person有确切答案或需要纠正时说一句，该找谁就 @ 谁
  • Suggest a card建议提卡nobody owns a to-do: one click turns it into a task有事没人认领，发一张一键转任务的建议卡
  • Wait先等一等a new question gets 3 minutes for a human to answer新问题先留 3 分钟给人回答
  • Nudge私信提醒needs someone who isn’t here: goes to their private assistant需要不在场的人，发到 TA 的私人助理和企微
  • Note it记一笔a decision was made: written to room memory, no message定下了一件事，悄悄写进本群记忆，不发消息
  1. Pause等停顿10 s by default; 3 s when called by name默认 10 秒，被叫名字只等 3 秒
  2. Jev scoresJev 打分last 20 messages, one call读最近 20 条，一次答完
  3. Room bar过门槛quiet 0.85 · normal 0.7 · active 0.6少说 0.85 / 适中 0.7 / 积极 0.6
  4. Rate limit频率与静音1–4 a minute; “stop” mutes for 2 h每分钟 1–4 次，「别插话」后静 2 小时
  5. Feedback看反馈reply = useful; 15 min of silence = ignored有人理算有用，15 分钟没人理算忽略

Shadow mode. Each way of stepping in, each rule and interjecting itself can be off, in shadow or live. In shadow the AI still decides and drafts, but only records what it would have done; members label drafts “should have said” or “shouldn’t have”, and once the labels are numerous and accurate it goes live in one click. Members write rules in one sentence (“no interjections after 8 pm”), which the model reads as trigger, forbid, require or quiet hours. Misses count too: if the AI stayed silent and was then @-ed about the same thing twice in a week, the bar drops.影子模式。每种介入方式、每条规则以及整个插话，都可以设成「关闭 / 影子 / 正式」。影子里 AI 照常判断、写好草稿，但只记录「本来会怎么做」；成员标注「该说 / 不该说」，标得够多够准就一键转正式。成员还能用一句话定规则（「晚上 8 点以后不插话」），模型读成触发、禁止、只在…时、免打扰四类。漏判也算：AI 没开口、之后 7 天内有人两次 @ 它问同一件事，门槛就调低。

02 · Task execution提卡执行

One lane to talk, another to workRouter + Worker 双轨：聊天和干活分开

The room’s AI only talks, checks facts and proposes task cards, with read-only access. A card says what to do, where, who owns it, when it’s due and how to tell it’s done. Once a person confirms, a separate execution agent (Codex by default, with GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek or Kimi behind it) opens a session for that card alone and reports back with evidence.群里的 AI 只负责对话、查证和提任务卡，权限只读。卡上写清做什么、在哪做、负责人、截止时间和「怎样算做完」。有人确认后，独立的执行 agent（默认 Codex，可选 GPT、Claude、Gemini、DeepSeek、Kimi）只为这张卡开一个会话，做完带证据汇报。

  1. Discuss讨论in the room or in WeCom在群里或企业微信里
  2. Propose提卡done-when criteria, review or not写明完成标准、要不要验收
  3. Confirm人确认anyone, members or admins所有人 / 成员 / 管理员可选
  4. Execute执行plan first, tick each step先列计划，做一步勾一步
  5. Evidence证据与验收test output, files, paths; rejections carry a reason测试输出、改动文件、产出路径；打回带原因

Router · the room’s AIRouter · 群里的 AI

One per room, always on. Read-only access to the workspace, memory and the web. The only voice in the room, and it keeps answering while tasks run.每群一个，常驻。只读工作区、记忆和网页。群里唯一说话的角色，任务跑着时照常问答。

Worker · the execution agentWorker · 执行 agent

One session per confirmed card, closed when done. Can edit files, run commands and browse. Never posts in the room; its progress lives on the card. Rooms run in parallel, cards in a room queue up.每张确认的卡一个会话，做完即结束。能读写文件、跑命令、联网。不在群里发言，过程收在任务卡里。不同群并行，同群排队。

Cards have a bar too: no card unless someone asked for the work, and none if three are already waiting, a similar one was dismissed in the past week, or it duplicates an open card. When a room is bound to Loom’s own code, the worker edits it on a separate branch and runs smoke tests; an admin clicks “deploy” to merge, and a failed health check rolls it back.提卡也有门槛：没人要它做就不提；已有 3 张待确认、同类卡 7 天内被忽略过、或和未完成的卡重复，都会拒绝。群绑定 Loom 自己的代码时，Worker 在独立分支里改、跑冒烟测试，管理员点「部署上线」才合并，健康检查失败自动回滚。

03 · Memory记忆

One memory tree, read in three layers一棵记忆树，分三层读

Decisions used to scatter across the chat, and the AI needed the background re-explained every time. Now stage summaries and memory live in one Markdown tree. Prompts carry only one-line abstracts (L0) and one-page overviews (L1); the AI opens the originals (L2) when it needs detail, so the context stays short and stable without losing anything.以前结论散在聊天里，AI 每次都要重新给背景。现在阶段总结和记忆合成一棵 Markdown 文件树：提示词里只放一句话摘要（L0）和一页概览（L1），需要细节时 AI 自己打开原文（L2）。上下文短而稳定，信息也不丢。

The tree记忆树

  • team/shared knowledge: background, who does what, decisions, in progress团队知识：背景、分工、决策约定、进行中
  • rooms/<room>/room notes, every stage summary, task log本群记忆、每次阶段总结、任务记录
  • people/<name>/profile and daily digests of private chats个人画像、和私人助理的每日摘要
  • daily/<date>.mdshort-term memory, one file a day每天一份短期记忆
  • history/old versions before rewrites and deletes; the AI can’t read them改写和删除前的旧版本，AI 读不到

Three layers三级分层

L0
one sentence per folder (≤ 80 chars); every reply carries the other rooms’ L0每个目录一句话（≤ 80 字）；每次回复带上其他群的 L0
L1
one page of decisions, work in progress and open items (≤ 1,500 chars); the team’s and this room’s一页要点：决定、进行中、待解决（≤ 1,500 字）；带团队和本群的 L1
L2
the originals, no limit; the AI browses, reads and searches them itself原文不限篇幅；AI 自己浏览、读取、检索

04 · Personal assistant私人助理

An assistant that learns you without being told私人助理：不用说「记住」，它也懂你

Each member gets a one-to-one assistant, on the web and in WeCom. Living in the same system as the team AI, it knows both you and your rooms: your tasks, your @-mentions, a morning brief under 300 characters at 9:30. After every turn it compares your profile with what you just said and keeps only what has clear evidence.每人一个只有自己能看的助理，网页和企业微信单聊都能用。它和团队 AI 在同一个系统里，所以既懂你，也知道你所在各群发生了什么：分给你的任务、@你的消息、每天 9:30 一份 300 字以内的早报。每轮对话后，它对比画像和刚才这轮，只记下有明确证据的信息。

05 · Engineering工程

Built fast, tested as if live做得快，也测得严

Python (FastAPI, SQLite) with a plain-JavaScript front end and no build step. I ran several coding agents in parallel, one each on the front end, the back end and the private assistant, and reviewed and merged their work. Changes go to an isolated instance first, and a deploy waits until the AI is idle.后端 Python（FastAPI + SQLite），前端原生 JavaScript，无构建步骤。开发时我同时带几个 coding agent 分工，前端交互、后端功能、私人助理各一个，由我审查合并。改动先在隔离实例上验证，部署时等 AI 空闲再切换。
